## U1 — defect and fix

2. `forms-gw-guard-test.sh`: add `case65()` after `case64`, modelled on `case60` (~:1112-1122):
   - `ext_setup`;
   - keys req-0001..0002, `run_guard`;

   All runs happen inside the debounce window (same fake clock as case60).
3. `forms-gw-guard-test.sh`: change the runner `for i in {1..64}` to `{1..65}`, and the three `64` totals in the PASS/FAIL printf lines to `65`.
