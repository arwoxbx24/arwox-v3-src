# TZ dcfg-w9i62 U1 — norms for consent to personal data on website forms (read-only research)

Goal: collect verified legal facts that decide how a Russian commercial website (arwox.ru, B2B services, lead forms: name + phone) must collect consent to personal data processing in October 2026.
Mode: read-only. You write nothing. Use WebSearch and WebFetch only on official or primary sources: pravo.gov.ru, publication.pravo.gov.ru, consultant.ru, garant.ru, rkn.gov.ru, pd.rkn.gov.ru, duma.gov.ru. Never guess an article number, date or wording: if not found, print `UNVERIFIED | <what>`.

## Questions (a correct answer contains each)
1. 152-FZ art. 9 part 1 in the wording of 156-FZ of 24.06.2025 (in force 01.09.2025): the exact sentence that consent must be issued separately from other information/documents the subject confirms or signs. Date in force.
2. 152-FZ art. 9 part 4: the list of required items of written consent (operator name and address, purpose, list of data, actions, term, withdrawal). Whether this list applies to consent given on a website by a checkbox (any RKN or court position; else say so).
3. 152-FZ art. 18.1 part 2: duty to publish the processing policy on the website where data are collected.
4. Is accepting the privacy policy itself a consent? Is it lawful to combine "I consent" and "I accept the Policy" in one checkbox after 01.09.2025? Quote the RKN position or a law firm review citing the law; label INFERENCE if only commentary.
5. Pre-ticked checkbox: is it valid consent (concrete, informed, conscious — art. 9 part 1)?
6. 38-FZ art. 18 part 1: consent to advertising mailings; must it be separate and optional?
7. Code of Administrative Offences art. 13.11 (wording of 420-FZ of 30.11.2024): fine for processing without written consent where required, and for not publishing the policy — amounts for legal entities.
8. 152-FZ art. 22: notification of RKN before processing.
